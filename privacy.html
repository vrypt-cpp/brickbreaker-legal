Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Scope

BrickBreaker Bot is a single-user automation tool. It operates only on the Google Account and YouTube channel of its owner. It is not offered as a service to the public and has no end users other than the operator.

Data we access

The application requests the youtube OAuth scope, which allows it to upload videos to the owner's channel. In practice it uses this scope only to:

  • upload a generated video,
  • read the upload/processing status of that video,
  • change the visibility of a video it uploaded itself.

Data we store

The application stores, on the owner's own server:

  • an OAuth refresh token, in a file readable only by the owner,
  • a local record of generated runs (seed, score, video id).

No data is transmitted to any third party. No analytics, tracking or advertising services are used.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, read or store personal information about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube users. We do not access comments, messages, or channel analytics.

Retention and deletion

The OAuth token is retained until the owner revokes it. It can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all access. Local records can be deleted by removing the application's data directory.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. Videos are uploaded directly from the owner's server to the YouTube Data API.

YouTube Terms of Service

Use of YouTube data is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.
